

## ANDROID TOOLING ALREADY PRESENT

This worker image already ships a complete Android/mobile toolkit: adb, fastboot,
aapt/aapt2, apksigner, zipalign, apktool, jadx, dex2jar, androguard, apkid,
apkleaks, quark, objection, frida (+ server binaries for all ABIs), gmsaas,
scrcpy, mitmproxy, tcpdump and nmap.

Before installing anything, check `command -v <tool>`. Do not reinstall tools
that are already present. A Genymotion Android 15 (arm64) VM can be booted and
connected with `android-vm up` (see /opt/android/README.md). Use
`android-vm down` to stop it when finished.
